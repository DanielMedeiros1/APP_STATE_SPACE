05 · Projeto da realimentação de estados

\[\mathcal{P}_d=\{-2 + j,\;-2 - j,\;-10\}\]
\[q_c(s)=\left(s-\left(-2 + j\right)\right)\left(s-\left(-2 - j\right)\right)(s + 10)\]
\[q_c(s)=s^{3} + 14s^{2} + 45s + 50\]
\[U=\begin{bmatrix}0 & 0 & -9 \\ 0 & -3 & 18 \\ 3 & -6 & 12\end{bmatrix}\]
\[U^{-1}=\begin{bmatrix}- \frac{8}{9} & - \frac{2}{3} & \frac{1}{3} \\ - \frac{2}{3} & - \frac{1}{3} & 0 \\ - \frac{1}{9} & 0 & 0\end{bmatrix}\]
\[A^{2}=\begin{bmatrix}13 & -24 & -3 \\ 8 & 13 & 6 \\ 0 & 0 & 4\end{bmatrix}\]
\[A^{3}=\begin{bmatrix}-28 & 135 & 30 \\ -45 & -28 & -25 \\ 0 & 0 & -8\end{bmatrix}\]
\[q_c(A)=A^{3}+(14)A^{2}+(45)A+(50)I\]
\[q_c(A)=\begin{bmatrix}24 & -66 & -12 \\ 22 & 24 & 14 \\ 0 & 0 & 8\end{bmatrix}\]
\[K=-e_n^TU^{-1}q_c(A)\]
\[e_n^TU^{-1}=\begin{bmatrix}- \frac{1}{9} & 0 & 0\end{bmatrix}\]
\[K=-\begin{bmatrix}- \frac{1}{9} & 0 & 0\end{bmatrix}\,\begin{bmatrix}24 & -66 & -12 \\ 22 & 24 & 14 \\ 0 & 0 & 8\end{bmatrix}\]
\[\boxed{K=\begin{bmatrix}\frac{8}{3} & - \frac{22}{3} & - \frac{4}{3}\end{bmatrix}}\]
\[A+BK=\begin{bmatrix}-4 & 3 & 0 \\ -1 & -4 & -1 \\ 8 & -22 & -6\end{bmatrix}\]
\[\det(sI-(A+BK))=s^{3} + 14s^{2} + 45s + 50\]
\[\lambda_{1} = -10,\quad \lambda_{2} = -2 - j,\quad \lambda_{3} = -2 + j\]

Conferência exata: o polinômio obtido coincide com o desejado.

Lei de controle: u = Kx; o sinal já está incorporado em K.
